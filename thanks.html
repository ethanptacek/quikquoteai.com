Here's what happens next.

Nothing about this is automated. Your message went straight into the same Atlas inbox QuikQuote's own clients use, and a real person — not a queue — reads it from there. Here's the shape of what typically happens from here.

  • Ethan reads it personally, usually the same day it arrives, and always within one business day — there's no support tier or ticket number involved, just a straight read of what you sent.
  • If anything about the project is unclear, you'll get a short reply with questions, plus an honest first read on rough scope, timeline and price based on similar builds.
  • If it looks like a fit, the next step is a short call to nail down the details, followed by a sitemap, a written scope and a fixed quote — before any design work starts.
  • There's no sales sequence, no newsletter, and no automated follow-up drip. Just this one reply, and then whatever you decide to do with it.
