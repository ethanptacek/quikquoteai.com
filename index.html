03 — The difference

A template gets you a website. Not a business.

Site builders hand you the keys — and the to-do list. A QuikQuote build keeps the to-do list on our side of the counter.

The old arrangement

A site you run yourself: plugins to patch, themes fighting each other, SEO left to chance, and leads scattered across three inboxes. Your evenings, forever.

The QuikQuote arrangement

A site that's run for you: hand-built pages, one login for everything, and Atlas agents watching SEO and security while you're out doing the actual work.
