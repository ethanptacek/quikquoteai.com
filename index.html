02 / The problem

A drag-and-drop template is not a growth plan.

Wix, Squarespace and a pile of WordPress plugins will get you a website. They won't get you found, they won't tell you what's working, and they leave the weekly upkeep on your desk.

Template builders & plugin stacks

  • Heavy, plugin-loaded pages that load slowly — and Google notices
  • Updates to install, plugins to patch, things that break on their own
  • SEO left to whatever the theme happened to ship with
  • Leads in one inbox, traffic in another tab, rankings nowhere
  • You're the one doing the work — every week, forever

A QuikQuote build

  • Hand-built pages, tuned for Core Web Vitals from day one
  • Nothing to patch — we host it, watch it, and keep it current
  • Structured for how Google and AI assistants actually read a page
  • Atlas puts your site, leads, SEO and analytics behind one login
  • We keep shipping after launch — it's not a hand-off and goodbye
